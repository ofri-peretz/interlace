`StatCard` and `StatGroup` get a `surface` prop. `card` (the default) is the bordered tile. `plain` drops the tile for a hairline rule above the number, so a few headline figures can sit on a page or a brand band like a line in a report. A plain stat takes its colour from what it sits on, label included, so it stays readable on `bg-primary`.

Components: stat-card, stat-group
Kind: Feature
