Section's `brand` band paints from new `--band` / `--band-foreground` tokens (`bg-band`, `text-band-foreground`). They follow the primary pair unless set, so nothing changes by default; a dark scheme can now keep its band deep while its primary is lightened for links and buttons. More-contrast mode puts the band back on its primary pair.

Components: section, interlace-base

Kind: Added
