New `List` primitive with markers that carry the voice of the page: `bullet`, `number` (a real `ol`, numbered by the browser in a filled circle), `check` and `cross`. Markers are drawn in CSS, so they centre on the first line whatever the item holds (rich text, inline `Citation` chips) and are never read aloud; `start` and `reversed` work as on any ordered list. `density` sets the space between items.
