New Drawer: a panel that slides in from an edge and follows the finger, built on Base UI's Drawer. Drag it toward its edge to dismiss it, let go early and it settles back, flick it and it leaves faster; the scrim thins as it goes. Bottom by default, with `direction` for top, right and left, and `snapPoints` for a bottom sheet that rests at a peek and at full height. Escape, a tap outside and a close button dismiss it too, focus is trapped and returned to the trigger, the body pads for the phone's home indicator, and the open and close slides are instant under reduced motion. Same part names as shadcn's Drawer.
