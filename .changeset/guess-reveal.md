GuessReveal: a one-question quiz. Two to four answers as buttons; the pick is marked right or wrong in words and with a short movement, the right answer is always shown, and an explanation opens. `onAnswer` lets the consumer record the pick. The status is a polite live region, the options stay focusable after the pick, and without JavaScript a details element holds the answer.
