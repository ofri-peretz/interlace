FlipCard: a card with a front that asks and a back that answers, turned over in 3D by a tap, a click or Enter. Both faces stay in the DOM and the card is as tall as its taller face, so a grid stays aligned. The turned-away face is inert and focus follows the turn; without JavaScript both faces show, and under reduced motion the faces cross-fade.
