Wordmark gains `size`: `md` (the default, unchanged), `sm` (about two thirds: text a step down, a thinner plate) and `responsive` (`sm` below the `md` breakpoint, `md` from it). A stacked three-line lockup in a sticky topbar took about a fifth of a phone's screen; `size="responsive"` gives that height back to the page.
