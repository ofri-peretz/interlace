`WeaveTexture`: a quiet woven cloth behind a section, in Interlace's two strand colours. Warp and weft cross in a plain weave, with the thread on top edged in the page colour so the crossing reads without shading. Nothing moves or shines: it is texture, not glow, and at the default strength text on top keeps its full contrast. Choose `density` (`fine`, `default`, `coarse`), `strength` (`faint`, `default`, `strong`) and `fade` (`none`, `edges`, `bottom`). A server component: one SVG pattern, nothing to hydrate.
