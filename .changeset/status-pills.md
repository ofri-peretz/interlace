New `StatusPills`: short facts about a page ("9 facts", "38 sources quoted") as a wrapping row of pills, made to sit on a brand band under a title. A default pill is an outline in the text colour around it, with no fill so its text keeps the band's contrast, so the same component reads on a dark band and on the page. `tone="ok"` is a solid pill with a check in the success colour, for a passed check.

Kind: Feature
