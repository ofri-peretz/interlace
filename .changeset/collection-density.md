`density` on the collections: `FeatureGrid`, `PricingTable`, `ArticleListGrid` and `StatGroup` take `compact`, `default` or `relaxed`. It sets the gap between items, the section's padding and each card's padding together, so a page tightens or opens up without a custom className. `default` renders exactly as before.
