New Reveal: any block arrives as it scrolls into view, once. `effect` picks how: `rise` (default) lifts it a line, `fade` fades it where it stands, `blur` brings it into focus, and `scale` grows it to size. `delay` sequences siblings and `amount` sets how much must be visible first. The content is in the server HTML, focus inside shows it at once, and under reduced motion it is simply there.
