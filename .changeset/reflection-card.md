A quiet minute in a loud room. `ReflectionCard` asks one prompt and keeps the answer private by default, saying so under the field. When the session allows it (`shareable`), the writer can tick to share it with the room without their name; only then is it checked like other room text (links, contact details, the blocklist), and a reflection that cannot be shared is still saved, with the reason said in words. With `Rating` before it, it makes an exit ticket.

Components: reflection-card
