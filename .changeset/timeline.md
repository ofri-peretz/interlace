New `Timeline`: a short record in date order, where each event has a date, a title, what was said and where it comes from. Once the timeline itself is 48rem wide the events sit in one row on a single rule; below that they stack down a vertical rule. The switch keys on the timeline's own width, not the viewport. `orientation="vertical"` always stacks, and `marker="accent"` fills one event's dot in the highlight colour.
