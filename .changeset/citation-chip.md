`Citation` takes `marker="chip"`: a small pill after the claim names the source ("UN", "Yale Avalon"), so the reader sees who backs a line before opening anything. The pill opens the same popover of sources and quotes. Its text defaults to the publishers, each once, joined with " · "; `chipLabel` shortens it. The visible text starts the accessible name, so voice users can say what they see.
