`Callout` gets a sixth tone, `highlight`, for the one line a page wants remembered: a highlighter wash behind the body and a highlighter rule on the left, with the body still in `text-foreground`. A new `kicker` slot puts a word or two in bold where the icon was ("In short", or a word in another script with its own `lang`). The kicker is text, so it is read before the body.
