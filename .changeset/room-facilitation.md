Four ways to run a live workshop, on the room's own state and transport. `PresenterNotes` is the presenter's second screen: notes per section, an agenda with the time planned and the time used, a clock, and a gentle over-time signal said in words and spoken once (never a flash or a sound). Space, the arrows, N, P and + drive it, and can be switched off; `useAgenda` keeps one window in control and any number showing the same agenda, applies a repeated tap once and drops one made against an older screen, and `createAgendaStore` restores it after a refresh with the clock still counting. `TeamBoard`, `TeamScoreboard` and `TeamBadge` make teams, balance them fairly (sizes within one; with scores the strongest players are dealt across teams), move a player by hand with a native select, and rank teams by average so a bigger team has no edge. `BreakoutHost` and `BreakoutCard` split the room into groups with a timer, send one message to every group (spoken once, whatever order states arrive in) and recall everyone. `PairHost` and `PairCard` pair everyone (a group of three when odd), deal each pair a card, and swap partners with the fewest repeats. `RoomClock` counts a host timestamp in minutes and seconds and speaks only a minute left, ten seconds and time up.

`RoomState` gains `shared`, named extras the host sets with a new `share` event, so teams, groups and pairs inherit the room's guarantees: the newest `seq` wins, a late joiner and a reconnecting phone get them with the state, and a restored host restores them. `serializeRoom`, `restoreRoom` and `createRoomStore` keep a room across the host's refresh (everyone comes back `away` and rejoins by themselves, `seq` moves ahead so phones take the saved room as the newest). `RoomTransport` and the in-memory and BroadcastChannel transports take a message type, so a presenter's windows reuse them. New tokens `--team-1` to `--team-5` (and `bg-team-N`, `border-team-N`) alias the chart palette and can be forked alone.
