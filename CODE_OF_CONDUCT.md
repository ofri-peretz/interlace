# Code of Conduct

Interlace follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique the work and not the person, and make
room for people who are new.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer at the address in
[`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove
comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may ban repeat offenders.
